## Our approach

negotimer ("the App") is a timer that helps run short pitches at networking
events, meetups and similar gatherings. It is available for iPhone, Android and Windows.

The data the App handles (your timer settings, your recordings and so on)
**stays entirely on your device and is never sent to us.**

There is one exception. **If you buy Pro for the Windows version**, we receive
and keep your name and email address in order to process the payment and send
you a license key (see "In-app purchases"). Unless you make that purchase,
we do not receive any information about you.

## Data the App handles

The App stores the following data, which you enter or create, on your device only:

- Timer run sheets (name, pitch time, question time, changeover time, number of speakers, etc.)
- App settings (display language, purchase status, etc.)
- Recorded audio files (only if you enable recording in Pro)

This data:

- **is never sent to our servers;**
- cannot be viewed by anyone else, including the developer;
- is deleted from your device when you uninstall the App.

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address,
phone number or location. There is no account registration or sign-in.
(Only if you buy Pro for the Windows version do we receive your name and email
address as part of the payment. See "In-app purchases".)

## Recording (Pro)

In Pro, you can record audio in sync with the timer.

- Recording happens only when you have allowed microphone access and turned recording on.
- Recorded audio files are **stored only in the App's private storage on your device**
  and are never sent automatically to our servers.
- Files are shared or deleted only by your own action. Once shared, how the data is
  handled depends on the service you chose to share it with.

⚠️ When recording at an event, please use it responsibly — for example,
**tell participants in advance that you are recording**. The App does not do this for you.

## The Windows version

The Windows version is for using the timer in online meetings.
This section explains how it differs from the phone versions.

### Showing the timer as a "camera"

To show the timer in your meeting tool, the Windows version
**registers the App in Windows' list of cameras**. Meeting tools see it
as an ordinary camera.

- What the App registers is **a video source that outputs images**.
  **It never reads video from any camera connected to your PC**, and the App
  never asks for camera permission.
- The only thing sent to the meeting tool is the timer image the App draws
  (time remaining, phase name and background colour).
- Registering requires administrator rights during installation. The registration
  is **removed when you uninstall the App.**

### Recording (Pro)

On Windows, recording **captures two sources together**:

- **your own voice** (audio from your microphone), and
- **audio your PC is playing** (the other meeting participants' voices, known as loopback recording).

The two are mixed into a single audio file.
In other words, **what other participants say is recorded too.**

- Recording happens only when you turn it on.
- Recorded files are **stored only on your PC** and are never sent automatically
  to our servers.
- Files are shared or deleted only by your own action. If you pass them to a
  generative AI service or similar, how the data is handled depends on the service you chose.

⚠️ **When recording an online meeting, tell every participant in advance that
you are recording and get their agreement.** A recording that includes other
people's words should never be made without telling them.
The App does not do this for you.

### Playing timer sounds into the meeting

You can choose to let meeting participants hear the timer's sound cues. In that case,
**the App only outputs the cues to the playback device you choose.**

- To reach the meeting, you need a **virtual audio device** that you install
  yourself (third-party software such as VB-CABLE). **It is not included with the App.**
- That software's behaviour and data handling are governed by its provider's terms.
- The App only outputs its built-in sound cues ("10 seconds left", "Time's up").
  It never sends your microphone audio.

### Joining a meeting

When you enter a meeting code, the App opens **a browser window dedicated to the timer**
and joins the meeting from that window.

- It connects to the meeting you entered (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, etc.)
  and does not go through our servers.
- Communication and data handling in that window are governed by
  **each meeting service's privacy policy**.
- Browser data used by that window (cookies, etc.) is kept in a dedicated location,
  separate from your regular browser, and only on your PC.

### Ads

**The Windows version shows no ads**, and no data is collected by AdMob.

## Advertising

The free version of the App shows ads provided by **Google AdMob (Google LLC)**.
To serve ads and measure their effectiveness, AdMob may collect data such as
device identifiers — including the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS,
advertising ID on Android) — and usage information.
For details, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

On iOS, an **App Tracking Transparency** prompt may appear, for example when you
first launch the App. If you do not allow tracking, ads are still shown but
**without tracking**. You can change this at any time in iOS
Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

**Once you buy Pro (a one-time purchase), ads are no longer shown and AdMob no
longer collects data through the App.**

## In-app purchases

Buying and restoring Pro (no ads + recording) is handled through the payment
systems of **the App Store (Apple)** or **Google Play (Google)**.
Payment information is managed by each store; we never have access to it.
The only thing the App receives is whether you have purchased.
Information related to payment is handled under each store's privacy policy.

Pro for the **Windows version** does not go through the App Store or Google Play.
Payment is processed by **Stripe (Stripe, Inc.)**, which manages your payment
information. We never have access to payment details such as card numbers.
Information related to payment is handled under
[Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

When your payment is complete, Stripe notifies us, and we automatically issue a
license key and send it to **the email address you used to pay**.
At that point we receive and store **your name, email address, the amount paid,
the payment identifier and the license key we issued**. We keep these to resend
keys and to answer your enquiries. We do not provide them to any third party.

License keys are **verified entirely on your PC**. The App never contacts our
servers to check them, and no internet connection is needed.

## Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your information to third parties except where required by law.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will announce it in the App or on the store page.
